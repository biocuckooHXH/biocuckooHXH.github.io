AI for Biological Discovery and Design

I am Xinhe (Olivia) Huang, a Ph.D. researcher in Biomedical Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Yu Xue.

My research focuses on agentic AI and foundation models for biological discovery, AI-guided protein design, and multimodal genomic learning. I connect biological data, machine learning, and experimental collaboration to develop computational models and testable biological hypotheses.

From February to July 2026, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Harvard AI and Robotics Lab, based at Massachusetts Eye and Ear, Harvard Medical School, in Boston, under the supervision of Prof. Mengyu Wang. There, I worked on multimodal genomic models integrating sequence and text information.

Postdoctoral Opportunities

I am seeking postdoctoral opportunities in 2027 in AI for biological discovery and design.

My research interests include:

  • Protein design: Using functional evidence to guide AI-driven protein design.
  • Agentic AI systems: Connecting biological knowledge, computational tools, and experimental feedback for hypothesis generation and refinement.
  • Biological foundation models and virtual cells: Integrating multimodal data to model cellular states and predict responses to perturbations.

I am also open to discussing any other related directions in computational biology.
